# EverMemory

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User requested Three.js and HTML. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with Three.js; local preview before any publishing.

## Product Purpose
A playable, explorable interpretation of Jiufen Old Street, Taiwan. Returning to places recovers a fictional character's childhood memories of family, food and familiar places.

## Users
Players who want a gentle adventure through a nostalgic mountain town. Desktop first, with mobile touch controls.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Name the character and choose male or female before exploring. Generic 3D character is explicitly acceptable. Connected lanes, proximity-based interactions, discoverable memories and a journal. Golden hour fading into lantern-lit dusk. Localhost first, no publishing authorised in this iteration.

## Brand Commitments
Ghibli-inspired atmosphere and Sakura Peak as a visual/gameplay reference. Jiufen's physical identity remains primary: hillside architecture, steep stairs, lanterns, tea houses and mountains. English interface with Traditional Chinese place signs. British English.

The user requested a warmer, more vibrant world after viewing the local build. Architecture uses terracotta, ochre, warm plaster and burgundy, with green concentrated in the foliage. Amber sunlight, peach haze and rose-toned dusk carry the atmosphere.

## Evidence on Hand
14 user-provided moodboard screenshots, including Jiufen architecture, maps and Sakura Peak. YouTube reference: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnzCwBJDI_A .

## Product Principles
- Places reveal the story through interaction.
- Exploration is unhurried; no combat or failure state assumed.
- This is a fictional, compressed town interpretation, not a navigation map.
- Save progress in the current browser; no account or backend needed for the local build.

## Open Decisions
Exact personal names and incidents are fictional authored content. Working title: The way home. No user-provided personal history is represented.
